Nemotron Speech Streaming 0.6B (int8)          models/nemotron/ — Windows, Linux
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Model:     nvidia/nemotron-speech-streaming-en-0.6b, revision
           ac0580bb7d3d6e39c4361db6afe28db9211793e4
           https://huggingface.co/nvidia/nemotron-speech-streaming-en-0.6b
Copyright: (c) NVIDIA Corporation
License:   NVIDIA Open Model License — https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/agreements/enterprise-software/nvidia-open-model-license/
           Licensed by NVIDIA Corporation under the NVIDIA Open Model License
           (version of October 24, 2025). Full text below.
Obtained:  https://github.com/smallbricktory/daisy-local/releases/tag/asr-nemotron-streaming-en-0.6b-560ms-int8-1
Files:     encoder.int8.onnx, encoder.int8.onnx.data, decoder.onnx,
           joiner.onnx, tokens.txt.

Changes to the licensed material: the files are a Derivative Model, an ONNX
export of NVIDIA's NeMo checkpoint made by Apexis Solutions Inc. (dba Small
Bricktory) with the sherpa-onnx export script
scripts/nemo/nemotron-speech-streaming-en-0.6b/export_onnx.py at commit
415b8c2aa261bcbe6c88772db9d80350955d1502 (Apache-2.0, Copyright 2026 Xiaomi
Corp.), adapted (scripts/nemotron-export/). The encoder weights are quantized
to per-channel uint8 (MatMul, Gemm and Conv except the subsampling stack); the
decoder and joiner are unquantized. The release carries the same notice and
the licence text as NOTICE.txt and LICENSE-NVIDIA-Open-Model-License.txt.
